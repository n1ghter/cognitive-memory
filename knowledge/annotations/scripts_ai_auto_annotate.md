## Business Logic & "Why"
This TypeScript file serves as a data ingestion and processing tool for an artificial intelligence (AI) model. The primary purpose of this script is to automate the process of annotating data with relevant information, enabling the AI model to learn and improve its performance.

The script utilizes various libraries and frameworks to connect with external data sources, retrieve data, and apply predefined annotation rules. The annotations generated by this script are intended to enhance the quality and relevance of the input data for the AI model.

The "Why" behind this script is to streamline the annotation process, reduce manual effort, and increase the efficiency of the AI model's training data preparation. By automating this step, the development team can focus on refining the AI model's performance rather than spending excessive time on data annotation.

## Architectural Role
This script plays a crucial role in the overall architecture of the project by serving as a critical component for data preparation and inputting high-quality data into the AI model. Its primary function is to facilitate the ingestion and processing of large datasets, ensuring that the data is accurately annotated and ready for use in training the AI model.

The script's modular design allows it to be easily integrated with other components of the project, making it a valuable asset for maintaining data quality and consistency throughout the development process.
